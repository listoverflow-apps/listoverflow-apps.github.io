Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

This policy applies to every app that links to this page.

Summary

These apps have no servers, no accounts of their own, no analytics and no ads. Anything an app stores stays on your device.

Google sign-in and Google APIs

Some apps let you sign in with Google to use your own data, for example read-only access to files in your Google Drive. When you sign in, Google shows exactly which access is requested. An app uses that access only for the feature you turned it on for, such as listing and downloading files you chose. Requests go directly from your device to Google; no other party receives your data.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data stored on your device

Files you download, your settings and your progress are stored only on your device. Sign-in credentials are kept on the device by Google's sign-in library. None of this is sent anywhere else.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in the app, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting an app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

These apps do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
